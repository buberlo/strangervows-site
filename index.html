Stranger Vows

A wedding-photo studio that stays on your iPhone.

Place a picture of yourself in a Chinese wedding set: a red-and-gold hall, a garden, a tea ceremony and other backdrops drawn in the app. Add a qipao, a xiuhe robe, a groom's jacket, props, frames and text.

Pose guides draw gold outlines over the camera. The fake couple generator draws a fictional pair for the call sheet. A shot list and checklist are stored on the device.

Three trial exports, then a one-time unlock. No account, no tracking, no data collected.
