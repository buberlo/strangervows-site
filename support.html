Support

There is no account to reset. Purchase, export and lists all live in the app on your iPhone.

Restore your purchase

The Full Unlock is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID. In Stranger Vows: More, then Restore Purchases. The same line is on the unlock screen. If the App Store does not answer, the app says so; reopening the app afterwards usually helps.

Trial exports

Without the unlock you get three exports with a watermark and a smaller set of backdrops and costumes. The unlock removes the cap and the watermark. There is no subscription.

Camera and photos

The camera is used for pose guides and stills. Saving asks iOS for permission to add a picture to Photos. If you declined, change it in Settings, Stranger Vows.

Person cut-out

The optional cut-out runs on the device with Apple's Vision framework. If it cannot run, the app falls back to a portrait matte.

Deleting your data

Removing the app deletes the shot list, checklist, trial counter and pictures that lived only inside the app. Pictures saved to Photos stay there until you delete them.

Fake couple

The couple generator builds a fictional pair from lists inside the app. It is not a photograph and not a real couple.
